# Printing Starfall with a grant

Use the Printing Legion link provided with your grant, or ask in Hack Club Slack #printing-legion for the current request flow. The public website was not accessible during this update; current checkout instructions and the account-specific grant balance could not be verified. Confirm the allowance shown in your grant rather than assuming an earlier quoted amount remains available.

1. Share this repository's production folder and DESIGN_NOTES.md with the printer.
2. Request black Top + Bottom; purple Middle_Purple + one Purple_Inlays_Print_Layout set. STL or STEP versions are equivalent geometry. Do not request the full electronics assembly as a single print.
3. Include production/fit_coupons and arrange switch/insert sample testing with your actual hardware before the full print. The 14.2 mm switch aperture is tight; adjust it if the sample requires. Tell the printer the inserts sit 6.9 mm below the support top.
4. Ask the printer to review orientation, thin accents and side trusses, then provide slicer-estimated total filament weight including supports and samples, plus shipping cost. Confirm those fit the grant's current limits and black/purple availability.
5. Use the payment or shipping link the program provides and the designated grant method. Do not send anyone your card details over Slack. Keep the receipt for your grant records.

The concept's keycap symbols and encoder knob are illustrative, not part of the case print order. Grant eligibility, remaining balance, expiration and checkout steps must be confirmed through your grant page/program contact.
