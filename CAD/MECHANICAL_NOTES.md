# Starfall mechanical notes (v25)

- Case outer size: 91.0 x 102.5 mm
- Total case stack height: 16.5 mm
- This version is taller than v24 by 2.6 mm to increase internal clearance.
- Top plate includes 3.4 mm M3 pass-through holes and 6.4 mm shallow head seats.
- Bottom includes 4.7 mm heat-set insert pockets for M3 threaded inserts.
- OLED header pins shortened to ~1.2 mm below the PCB plane.
- USB-C opening included.
- Production files are the 4 case parts in /production.

Submission note: this package still needs your final KiCad project files inside /PCB and your own README before final Hackpad submission.
